% ---------- Sixteenth Poem ----------
\poemhead{CAROL OF THE BIRDS}

\begin{verse}
Whence comes this rush of wings afar.\\
Following straight the Noël star?\\
Birds from the woods in wondrous flight,\\
Bethlehem seek this Holy Night.\\[0.7em]

"Tell us, ye birds, why come ye here.\\
Into this stable, poor and drear?"\\
"Hast'ning we seek the new-born King,\\
And all our sweetest music bring."\\[0.7em]

Hark how the green-finch bears his part,\\
Philomel, too, with tender heart,\\
Chants from her leafy dark retreat\\
Re, mi, fa, sol, in accents sweet.\\[0.7em]

Angels and shepherds, birds of the sky,\\
Come where the Son of God doth lie;\\
Christ on the earth with man doth dwell.\\
Join in the shout, Noël, Noël.%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{Bas-Quercy}
