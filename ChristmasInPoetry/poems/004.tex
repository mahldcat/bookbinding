% ---------- Fourth Poem ----------
\poemtitle{'TWAS JOLLY, JOLLY WAT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{'TWAS JOLLY, JOLLY WAT}

\begin{verse}
'Twas jolly, jolly Wat, my foy,\\
He was a goodman's shepherd boy,\\
\hspace*{2em}And he sat by his sheep\\
\hspace*{2em}On the hill-side so steep,\\
\hspace*{4em}And piped this song,\\
\hspace*{6em}Ut hoy! Ut hoy!\\
\hspace*{2em}O merry, merry sing for joy,\\
\hspace*{6em}Ut hoy!\\[0.7em]

A'down from Heav'n that is so high\\
There came an angel companye,\\
\hspace*{2em}And on Bethlehem hill\\
\hspace*{2em}Thro' the night-tide so still\\
\hspace*{4em}Their song out-rang:\\
\hspace*{6em}On high, On high,\\
\hspace*{2em}O glory be to God on high,\\
\hspace*{6em}On high!\\[0.7em]

Now must Wat go where Christ is born,\\
Yea, go and come again to-morn.\\
\hspace*{2em}And my pipe it shall play,\\
\hspace*{2em}All my heart it doth say\\
\hspace*{4em}To Shepherd King:\\
\hspace*{6em}Ut hoy! Ut hoy!\\
\hspace*{2em}O merry, merry sing for joy,\\
\hspace*{6em}Ut hoy!\\[0.7em]

O peace on earth, good will to men,\\
The angels sang again, again,\\
\hspace*{2em}For to you was He born\\
\hspace*{2em}On this Christmas morn,\\
\hspace*{4em}So sing we all:\\
\hspace*{6em}On high, On high,\\
\hspace*{2em}O glory be to God on high,\\
\hspace*{6em}On high!\\[0.7em]

Jesu my King, it's naught for Thee,\\
A bob of cherries, one, two, three,\\
\hspace*{2em}But my tar-box and ball,\\
\hspace*{2em}And my pipe, I give all\\
\hspace*{4em}To Thee, my King.\\
\hspace*{6em}Ut hoy! Ut hoy!\\
\hspace*{2em}O merry, merry sing for joy,\\
\hspace*{6em}Ut hoy!\\[0.7em]

Farewell, herd-boy, saith Mary mild.\\
Thanks, jolly Wat, smiled Mary's child,\\
\hspace*{2em}For fit gift for a king\\
\hspace*{2em}Is your heart in the thing.\\
\hspace*{4em}So pipe you well,\\
\hspace*{6em}For joy, for joy!\\
\hspace*{2em}O merry, merry sing for joy,\\
\hspace*{6em}Ut hoy!%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{C. W. Stubbs}
