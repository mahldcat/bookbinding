% ---------- Tenth Poem ----------
\poemhead{A CHRISTMAS HYMN}

\begin{verse}
Once in royal David's city\\
\hspace*{2em}Stood a lowly cattle-shed\\
Where a mother laid her Baby,\\
\hspace*{2em}In a manger for His bed.\\
Mary was that mother mild,\\
Jesus Christ her little Child.\\[0.7em]

He came down to earth from heaven,\\
\hspace*{2em}Who is God and Lord of all,\\
And His shelter was a stable,\\
\hspace*{2em}And His cradle was a stall.\\
With the poor, and mean, and lowly\\
Lived on earth our Saviour holy.\\[0.7em]

And through all His wondrous childhood,\\
\hspace*{2em}He would honour and obey.\\
Love and watch the lowly mother\\
\hspace*{2em}In whose gentle arms He lay.\\
Christian children, all must be\\
Mild, obedient, good as He.\\[0.7em]

For He is our childhood's Pattern,\\
\hspace*{2em}Day by day like us He grew;\\
He was little, weak, and helpless,\\
\hspace*{2em}Tears and smiles like us He knew:\\
And He feeleth for our sadness,\\
And He shareth in our gladness.\\[0.7em]

And our eyes at last shall see Him,\\
\hspace*{2em}Through His own redeeming love,\\
For that Child so dear and gentle\\
\hspace*{2em}Is our Lord in Heaven above;\\
And He leads His children on\\
To the place where He is gone.\\[0.7em]

Not in that poor lowly stable,\\
\hspace*{2em}With the oxen standing by,\\
We shall see Him; but in Heaven,\\
\hspace*{2em}Set at God's right hand on high;\\
When like stars His children crowned,\\
All in white shall wait around.%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{C. Frances Alexander}
