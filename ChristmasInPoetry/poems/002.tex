% ---------- Second Poem ----------
\poemhead{FROM FAR AWAY}

\begin{verse}
From far away we come to you.\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door,\\
To tell of great tidings, strange and true.\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\
\hspace*{4em}From far away we come to you,\\
\hspace*{4em}To tell of great tidings, strange and true.\\[0.7em]

For as we wandered far and wide,\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door,\\
What hap do you deem there should us betide?\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\[0.7em]

Under a bent when the night was deep,\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door,\\
There lay three shepherds, tending their sheep.\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\[0.7em]

"O ye shepherds, what have ye seen,\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door,\\
To stay your sorrow and heal your teen?"\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\[0.7em]

"In an ox stall this night we saw,\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door,\\
A Babe and a maid without a flaw.\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\[0.7em]

"There was an old man there beside;\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door,\\
His hair was white, and his hood was wide.\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\[0.7em]

"And as we gazed this thing upon,\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door,\\
Those twain knelt down to the little one.\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\[0.7em]

"And a marvellous song we straight did hear,\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door.\\
That slew our sorrow and healed our care."\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\[0.7em]

News of a fair and marvellous thing,\\
\hspace*{2em}The snow in the street, and the wind on the door,\\
Nowell, Nowell, Nowell, we sing.\\
\hspace*{2em}Minstrels and maids, stand forth on the floor.\\
\hspace*{4em}From far away we come to you,\\
\hspace*{4em}To tell of great tidings, strange and true.%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{William Morris}
