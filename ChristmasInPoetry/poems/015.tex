% ---------- Fifteenth Poem ----------
\poemtitle{CHRISTMAS EVE}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{CHRISTMAS EVE}

\begin{verse}
In holly hedges starving birds\\
\hspace*{2em}Silently mourn the setting year;\\
Upright like silver-plated swords\\
\hspace*{2em}The flags stand in the frozen mere.\\[0.7em]

The mistletoe we still adore\\
\hspace*{2em}Upon the twisted hawthorn grows:\\
In antique gardens hellebore\\
\hspace*{2em}Puts forth its blushing Christmas rose.\\[0.7em]

Shrivell'd and purple, cheek by jowl,\\
\hspace*{2em}The hips and haws hang drearily;\\
Roll'd in a ball the sulky owl\\
\hspace*{2em}Creeps far into his hollow tree.\\[0.7em]

In abbeys and cathedrals dim\\
\hspace*{2em}The birth of Christ is acted o'er;\\
The kings of Cologne worship him,\\
\hspace*{2em}Balthazar, Jasper, Melchior.\\[0.7em]

The shepherds in the field at night\\
\hspace*{2em}Beheld an angel glory-clad.\\
And shrank away with sore afright.\\
\hspace*{2em}"Be not afraid," the angel bade.\\[0.7em]

"I bring good news to king and clown,\\
\hspace*{2em}To you here crouching on the sward;\\
For there is born in David's town\\
\hspace*{2em}A Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.\\[0.7em]

"Behold the babe is swathed, and laid\\
\hspace*{2em}Within a manger." Straight there stood\\
Beside the angel all arrayed\\
\hspace*{2em}A heavenly multitude.\\[0.7em]

"Glory to God," they sang; "and peace,\\
\hspace*{2em}Good pleasure among men."\\
The wondrous message of release!\\
\hspace*{2em}Glory to God again!\\[0.7em]

Hush! Hark! the waits, far up the street!\\
\hspace*{2em}A distant, ghostly charm unfolds,\\
Of magic music wild and sweet,\\
\hspace*{2em}Anemones and clarigolds.%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{John Davidson}\\
\noindent\textit{From \emph{Fleet Street Eclogues.} Included by permission of Dodd, Mead and Company.}
