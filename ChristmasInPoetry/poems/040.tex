% ---------- Fortieth Poem ----------
\poemtitle{OUR JOYFUL FEAST}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{OUR JOYFUL FEAST}

\begin{verse}
So, now is come our joyful feast,\\
\hspace*{2em}Let every soul be jolly!\\
Each room with ivy leaves is drest,\\
\hspace*{2em}And every post with holly.\\
Though some churls at our mirth repine,\\
Round your brows let garlands twine,\\
Drown sorrow in a cup of wine,\\
\hspace*{2em}And let us all be merry!\\[0.7em]

Now all our neighbours' chimneys smoke,\\
\hspace*{2em}And Christmas logs are burning;\\
Their ovens with baked meats do choke,\\
\hspace*{2em}And all their spits are turning.\\
Without the door let sorrow lie,\\
And if for cold it hap to die,\\
We'll bury it in Christmas pie,\\
\hspace*{2em}And evermore be merry!%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{George Wither}
