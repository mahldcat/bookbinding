% ---------- Seventeenth Poem ----------
\poemtitle{THE SHEPHERDS HAD AN ANGEL}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{THE SHEPHERDS HAD AN ANGEL}

\begin{verse}
The shepherds had an angel,\\
\hspace*{2em}The wise men had a star;\\
But what have I, a little child,\\
\hspace*{2em}To guide me home from far,\\
Where glad stars sing together,\\
\hspace*{2em}And singing angels are?\\[0.7em]

Lord Jesus is my Guardian,\\
\hspace*{2em}So I can nothing lack;\\
The lambs lie in His bosom\\
\hspace*{2em}Along life's dangerous track:\\
The wilful lambs that go astray\\
\hspace*{2em}He, bleeding, brings them back.\\[0.7em]

Those shepherds thro' the lonely night\\
\hspace*{2em}Sat watching by their sheep,\\
Until they saw the heav'nly host\\
\hspace*{2em}Who neither tire nor sleep,\\
All singing Glory, glory,\\
\hspace*{2em}In festival they keep.\\[0.7em]

Christ watches me, His little lamb,\\
\hspace*{2em}Cares for me day and night,\\
That I may be His own in heav'n;\\
\hspace*{2em}So angels clad in white\\
Shall sing their Glory, glory,\\
\hspace*{2em}For my sake in the height.\\[0.7em]

Lord, bring me nearer day by day,\\
\hspace*{2em}Till I my voice unite,\\
And sing my Glory, glory,\\
\hspace*{2em}With angels clad in white.\\
All Glory, glory, giv'n to Thee,\\
\hspace*{2em}Thro' all the heav'nly height.%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{Christina G. Rossetti}
