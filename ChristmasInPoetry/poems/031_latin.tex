% ---------- Thirty-first Poem ----------
\poemhead{GOOD KING WENCESLAS}

\begin{verse}
Good King Wenceslas looked out\\
\hspace*{2em}On the Feast of Stephen,\\
When the snow lay round about,\\
\hspace*{2em}Deep, and crisp, and even.\\[0.7em]

Brightly shone the moon that night\\
\hspace*{2em}Though the frost was cruel,\\
When a poor man came in sight,\\
\hspace*{2em}Gath'ring winter fuel.\\[0.7em]

"Hither, page, and stand by me,\\
\hspace*{2em}If thou know'st it, telling.\\
Yonder peasant, who is he?\\
\hspace*{2em}Where and what his dwelling?"\\[0.7em]

"Sire, he lives a good league hence,\\
\hspace*{2em}Underneath the mountain;\\
Right against the forest fence,\\
\hspace*{2em}By Saint Agnes' fountain."\\[0.7em]

"Bring me flesh, and bring me wine,\\
\hspace*{2em}Bring me pine-logs hither;\\
Thou and I shall see him dine,\\
\hspace*{2em}When we bear them thither."\\[0.7em]

Page and monarch, forth they went,\\
\hspace*{2em}Forth they went together;\\
Through the rude wind's wild lament\\
\hspace*{2em}And the bitter weather.\\[0.7em]

"Sire, the night is darker now,\\
\hspace*{2em}And the wind blows stronger;\\
Fails my heart, I know not how,\\
\hspace*{2em}I can go no longer."\\[0.7em]

"Mark my footsteps, good my page;\\
\hspace*{2em}Tread thou in them boldly:\\
Thou shalt find the winter rage\\
\hspace*{2em}Freeze thy blood less coldly."\\[0.7em]

In his master's steps he trod,\\
\hspace*{2em}Where the snow lay dinted;\\
Heat was in the very sod\\
\hspace*{2em}Where the saint has printed.\\[0.7em]

Therefore, Christian men, be sure,\\
\hspace*{2em}Wealth or rank possessing,\\
Ye who now will bless the poor,\\
\hspace*{2em}Shall yourselves find blessing.%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{Translated from the Latin by J. M. Neale}
