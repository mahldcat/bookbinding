% ---------- Twenty-ninth Poem ----------
\poemhead{A CHRISTMAS CAROL}

\begin{verse}
There's a song in the air!\\
There's a star in the sky!\\
There's a mother's deep prayer\\
And a baby's low cry!\\
\hspace*{2em}And the star rains its fire while the Beautiful sing,\\
\hspace*{2em}For the manger of Bethlehem cradles a king.\\[0.7em]

There's a tumult of joy\\
O'er the wonderful birth,\\
For the virgin's sweet boy\\
Is the Lord of the earth,\\
\hspace*{2em}Ay! the star rains its fire and the Beautiful sing,\\
\hspace*{2em}For the manger of Bethlehem cradles a king.\\[0.7em]

In the light of that star\\
Lie the ages impearled;\\
And that song from afar\\
Has swept over the world.\\
\hspace*{2em}Every hearth is aflame, and the Beautiful sing\\
\hspace*{2em}In the homes of the nations that Jesus is King.\\[0.7em]

We rejoice in the light,\\
And we echo the song\\
That comes down through the night\\
From the heavenly throng.\\
\hspace*{2em}Ay! we shout to the lovely evangel they bring,\\
\hspace*{2em}And we greet in His cradle our Saviour and King.%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{Josiah Gilbert Holland}
