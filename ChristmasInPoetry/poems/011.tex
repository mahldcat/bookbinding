% ---------- Eleventh Poem ----------
\poemtitle{CHRISTMAS}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{CHRISTMAS}

\begin{verse}
While shepherds watch'd their flocks by night,\\
\hspace*{2em}All seated on the ground,\\
The angel of the Lord came down,\\
\hspace*{2em}And glory shone around.\\[0.7em]

"Fear not," said he (for mighty dread\\
\hspace*{2em}Had seized their troubled mind);\\
"Glad tidings of great joy I bring\\
\hspace*{2em}To you and all mankind.\\[0.7em]

"To you, in David's town, this day\\
\hspace*{2em}Is born of David's line\\
The Saviour who is Christ the Lord;\\
\hspace*{2em}And this shall be the sign:\\[0.7em]

"The heavenly Babe you there shall find\\
\hspace*{2em}To human view display'd,\\
All meanly wrapt in swathing bands,\\
\hspace*{2em}And in a manger laid."\\[0.7em]

Thus spake the Seraph; and forthwith\\
\hspace*{2em}Appear'd a shining throng\\
Of angels, praising God, and thus\\
\hspace*{2em}Address'd their joyful song:\\[0.7em]

"All glory be to God on high,\\
\hspace*{2em}And to the earth be peace;\\
Good-will henceforth from heaven to men\\
\hspace*{2em}Begin, and never cease!"%
\end{verse}

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textit{Nahum Tate}
